About

I'm a developer who likes projects with a clear, narrow job. Most of what I build starts as something I needed myself: a way to track expenses, a faster way to search old notes, a script that saved me an afternoon.

Before this I spent six years building internal tools at a local newsroom, which is where I learned that the best software is the kind nobody has to think about.
